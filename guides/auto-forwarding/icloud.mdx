---
title: "Forward iCloud Mail to Mermail"
description: "Forward all or selected iCloud Mail messages to a Mermail mailbox."
icon: "apple"
---

iCloud Mail can forward all incoming mail or use rules to forward selected messages. Use a rule when you want to limit what Mermail AI can process.

## Prerequisites

- A Mermail mailbox address.
- An active iCloud Mail account.
- Access to [iCloud Mail](https://www.icloud.com/mail) in a browser.

## Forward selected messages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open iCloud Mail settings">
    Sign in to iCloud Mail, click the settings button above the mailbox list, and choose **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open rules">
    Select **Rules**, then add a rule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the messages">
    Choose criteria such as sender, recipient, or subject that identify mail you want Mermail to process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose forwarding">
    Set the rule action to forward the message to your Mermail mailbox address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
    Save the rule and send a new matching message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Each iCloud Mail forwarding rule can use one forwarding address.
</Info>

## Forward all messages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Mail Forwarding">
    In iCloud Mail settings, select **Mail Forwarding**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable forwarding">
    Select **Forward my email to** and enter the Mermail mailbox address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the iCloud copy">
    Leave **Delete messages after forwarding** cleared unless you intentionally want iCloud to remove its copy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting messages after forwarding removes the provider copy. Keeping it gives you an independent record in iCloud Mail and makes human takeover easier.
</Warning>

## Test the setup

Send a new message that matches the rule. Confirm that it appears in both iCloud Mail and Mermail. If you created a selective rule, send a second excluded message and confirm that it remains only in iCloud Mail.

## Stop forwarding

- Delete or disable the selective rule in iCloud Mail settings.
- For account-wide forwarding, open **Mail Forwarding** and clear **Forward my email to**.

## Human takeover

If you reply to a forwarded thread directly in iCloud Mail, Mermail treats the entire thread as human-managed and permanently stops AI processing for it.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| New mail is not forwarded | Confirm the forwarding address and rule criteria |
| Old mail is not forwarded | Automatic forwarding applies to new incoming messages |
| iCloud storage keeps growing | Review whether provider copies should be archived or deleted |
| Sensitive messages reach Mermail | Replace account-wide forwarding with a narrow rule |

For current provider instructions, see [Automatically forward email in Mail on iCloud.com](https://support.apple.com/en-vn/guide/icloud/mm6b1a3960/icloud).
