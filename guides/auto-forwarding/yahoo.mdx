---
title: "Forward Yahoo Mail to Mermail"
description: "Configure Yahoo Mail forwarding to a Mermail mailbox when the feature is available."
icon: "envelope"
---

Yahoo Mail forwarding is available with Yahoo Mail Plus and is not available in every locale. The setting may not appear in your account.

## Prerequisites

- A Yahoo Mail Plus subscription.
- A Mermail mailbox address.
- A locale and account where Yahoo exposes automatic forwarding.

## Enable forwarding

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Yahoo settings">
    In Yahoo Mail, click **Settings** and then **More Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the mailbox">
    Click **Mailboxes** and select the primary mailbox account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the destination">
    Under **Forwarding** or **Auto-forwarding**, enter the Mermail mailbox address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the address">
    Click **Verify**, then open the message delivered to Mermail and follow its instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test new mail">
    Send a new message to the Yahoo address and confirm that it appears in Mermail.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Yahoo’s published forwarding setup is account-wide. Do not assume selective forwarding is available unless your current Yahoo Mail interface provides supported rule conditions for it.
</Warning>

If the account contains messages that should not reach AI, use a dedicated Yahoo address for the Mermail workflow or choose a provider that supports conditional forwarding.

## Stop forwarding

Return to **Settings** → **More Settings** → **Mailboxes**, select the primary mailbox, and remove or disable the forwarding address.

## Human takeover

If you reply to a forwarded thread directly in Yahoo Mail, Mermail treats the entire thread as human-managed and permanently stops AI processing for it.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Forwarding is not listed | Confirm your Mail Plus subscription and locale availability |
| Forwarding is not active | Complete the verification instructions delivered to Mermail |
| Private messages reach Mermail | Disable account-wide forwarding and use a dedicated source mailbox |
| New mail is delayed | Check both Yahoo and Mermail spam folders before retrying |

For current provider instructions, see [Enable automatic email forwarding in Yahoo Mail](https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN29133.html).
