**Your AI can now sign up and pay.**

Mermail gives your agent a private email and a user-controlled Agent Wallet, so
it can sign up for services, receive verification codes, pay online, and keep
every receipt. One MCP connection gives your agent the tools to complete the
workflow from inbox to payment.

<Info>
  You stay in control. Your agent can prepare email and payment actions, but
  sending email and moving funds require your approval.
</Info>

## How Mermail fits into a workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an agent inbox">
    Create a hosted Mermail address or use a verified custom domain. Your agent
    can receive email, search conversations, and prepare replies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your AI">
    Connect Mermail through MCP, install a focused Agent Skill, or use the CLI
    for a repeatable workflow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add controlled payments">
    Connect PayBox to Agent Wallet when your agent needs to fund a wallet or pay
    in USDC. You review each transfer before funds move.
  </Step>
</Steps>
