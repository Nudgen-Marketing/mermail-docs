Plan limits apply to each workspace.

| Plan       | Price     | Mailboxes | Members | Email quota | Storage | Custom domains |
| ---------- | --------- | --------: | ------: | ----------: | ------: | -------------: |
| Free       | $0/month  |         2 |       2 |       1,000 |    3 GB |              0 |
| Developer  | $10/month |        10 |       5 |      10,000 |   10 GB |              5 |
| Enterprise | Custom    |    Custom |  Custom |      Custom |  Custom |         Custom |

## Developer checkout

Developer checkout is handled through Polar. The app stores subscription state by workspace and updates it from Polar webhooks.

## Custom domains

Custom domains are paid-plan features. The Developer plan includes 5 custom domains. Enterprise limits are negotiated.
