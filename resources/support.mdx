Contact the Mermail team when you need help with your account, workspace, or
mailboxes. You can also join the community to ask questions and share feedback.

  <Card title="Email support" icon="envelope" href="mailto:contact@mermail.app">

    questions.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Join Discord" icon="discord" href="https://discord.gg/Mkw5Kmpt">
    Ask questions, talk with other Mermail users, and share product feedback in
    the Mermail Discord community.
  </Card>

## Before you contact support

Include the following details when they apply:

- Your workspace name.
- The affected mailbox address.
- What you expected to happen.
- What happened instead.
- Any error message you received.

<Warning>
  Do not send passwords, access tokens, API keys, private email content, or other
  secrets through email or Discord.
</Warning>
