Mermail evaluates customer-controlled storage and zero data retention
requirements for qualified Enterprise accounts. A workspace is not covered by
zero data retention unless Mermail confirms the configuration and its scope in
writing.

<Warning>
  This page describes the requirements for a zero data retention configuration.
  It is not a statement that zero data retention is currently enabled or
  generally available.
</Warning>

## What customer content includes

For a zero data retention review, customer content includes:

- Email message bodies, headers, and attachments.
- Drafts, replies, and other generated mailbox content.
- Agent prompts, model inputs, and model outputs.
- Agent conversations and retrieved context.
- Connected-tool inputs and results.

Account identifiers, billing records, security events, usage measurements, and
audit metadata are operational data. A written Enterprise agreement must define
which operational data Mermail retains and for how long.

## Proposed scope

A customer-controlled storage configuration is intended to separate real-time
processing from persistent content storage. When confirmed in writing:

- Customer content is processed only to provide the requested mailbox or agent
  operation.
- Persistent customer content is written to the approved customer-controlled
  storage boundary.
- Mermail does not use customer content to train shared models.
- Any temporary copies, queues, caches, logs, or derived content follow the
  retention limits in the Enterprise agreement.

## What is not covered

Zero data retention does not automatically remove all operational records. The
written scope may exclude data needed for:

- **Account and organization data:** Authentication, workspace administration,
  and support.
- **Billing and usage data:** Metering, invoices, limits, and capacity planning.
- **Security and audit events:** Access review, abuse prevention, incident
  investigation, and compliance evidence.
- **Delivery metadata:** Routing, suppression, bounce, complaint, and
  deliverability operations.
- **Legal records:** Compliance with applicable law and binding legal
  obligations.

The Enterprise agreement must identify each retained category, its fields,
storage location, access controls, and retention period.

## Third-party providers

Customer-controlled storage does not by itself control every external service.
Email delivery, model inference, observability, support, and connected tools may
process customer content or operational data.

Before enablement, Mermail and the customer must verify:

- Which providers receive customer content.
- The provider's retention and training terms.
- Whether a zero-retention or equivalent provider configuration is available.
- Data residency and cross-border transfer requirements.
- Subprocessor and deletion obligations.

<Info>
  Data sent directly to a third-party integration follows that provider's terms
  unless a written agreement states otherwise.
</Info>

## Feature availability

Mailbox features that depend on persistent content require working
customer-controlled storage. A feature may be unavailable when its data cannot
remain within the approved boundary.

The enablement review must confirm support for:

- Message history, search, and thread views.
- Attachments, drafts, and scheduled sends.
- Agent conversation history and retrieval.
- Task triage, memory, and RAG documents.
- Support diagnostics, exports, and backups.

If a feature cannot meet the approved storage and retention boundary, Mermail
must disable it or document the exception before enablement.

## Legal, security, and abuse-prevention exceptions

A zero data retention agreement may allow limited retention when required by
law, needed to investigate a security incident, or necessary to prevent abuse.
Any exception must define its trigger, access controls, retention limit, and
deletion process.

Mermail must not describe an exception as unlimited or use it to retain customer
content for unrelated product development or model training.

## Request an Enterprise review

Email [contact@mermail.app](mailto:contact@mermail.app) to request an Enterprise
data-handling review. Include:

- The customer-controlled storage environment you want to use.
- Required regions and data residency.
- Required retention and deletion periods.
- Mailbox and agent features you need.
- Model and third-party provider restrictions.
- Your GDPR, security, and audit requirements.

Mermail must confirm eligibility, scope, limitations, providers, and exceptions
in writing before you treat a workspace as zero data retention.
