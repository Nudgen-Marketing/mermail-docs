Use [Site Analytics](/guides/site-analytics) to turn the public information on
your website into shared reference material for your support mailbox agents.
Review the findings, apply them to the right workspace, choose how replies are
handled, and give selected mailboxes a consistent signature.

## Example: support for an online store

You run an online store. Customers ask about products, delivery, and returns,
but the answers are spread across product pages, an FAQ, and policy pages.
You want your support mailbox agents to reference the same business information
without copying it into each mailbox separately.

In this example, you apply the store's website to its workspace and add a
branded signature to its support mailbox. A separate partnerships mailbox keeps
its existing signature.

<Note>
  Business information is shared across mailbox agents in the workspace.
  Selecting mailboxes for a signature does not limit which agents can use it.
  Use separate workspaces for brands that need separate website profiles.
</Note>

## Before you begin

- Have a public website with the business information you want to use.
- Confirm you have admin access to the store's Mermail workspace.
- [Create a support mailbox](/guides/create-mailbox) or choose an existing one.
- If you want a branded email address, complete [custom domain setup](/guides/custom-domains)
  and wait until the domain shows **Sending ready** and **Receiving ready**.
  Workspace mailboxes then move to the domain automatically.

You can run this setup in the Mermail web app. It does not require an external
MCP connection or a paid add-on.

## Set up the workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Analyze the store's website">
    On the Mermail home page, select the store's workspace. On the
    **Site Analytics** card, click **Open Site Analytics**. Enter your public website address under
    **Website address** and select **Analyze website**.

    Use the website whose information should apply to this workspace. The scan
    reads accessible public content, not private order records or customer
    accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the information customers rely on">
    When the analysis is ready, select **Review and apply** and read the brand
    summary. Compare important findings with their source pages.

    For the store, check that delivery and return information matches the
    published policies. Do not treat an absent policy as permission to offer
    a refund or promise a delivery date. A partial result can still be useful,
    but it does not confirm that every page or policy was included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the knowledge to the store's workspace">
    Select **Apply to workspace**. If the workspace already uses another
    website or a manually entered profile, review the replacement and select
    **Replace and apply**.

    Under **Active business information**, confirm the website and that it is
    **Enabled**.
    The workspace's mailbox agents can now reference its applied business
    information. Applying context does not send a customer email or activate
    the suggested signature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize the support signature">
    Under **Website signature** on the same page, select **Edit signature**. Review the
    suggested contact details and adjust the wording for your support team.

    Select **Save changes** to update the preview. Under **Apply to mailboxes**,
    select the support mailbox and leave the partnerships mailbox unchecked.
    Select **Review and apply** and confirm any intended overwrite.

    Only the selected mailboxes receive this signature. Saving inside the
    editor alone does not persist it; a successful Apply does. Reload the page
    to check the saved version, then open a new compose in the support mailbox
    to inspect the signature without sending a message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how the support mailbox replies">
    Open the support mailbox's settings and go to **Email response mode**.

    - Start with **Draft for review** so you can check the first replies before sending them. Save this choice before testing.
    - Choose **Automatic triage** to send routine replies automatically, such
      as product, pricing, how-to, and shipping or return policy questions
      answered by the business information you applied.

    With **Automatic triage**, set **Business type** to **Ecommerce** for the
    store. Security, account access, refunds, cancellations, disputes, serious
    complaints, and uncertain answers always wait for review. Under
    **Additional review topics**, add store-specific topics such as
    `Custom shipping requests`, one per line.

    Optionally, under **Customer status lookups**, connect the store's order
    account so replies can include order status. Replies based on public
    information work without a connection. Save your changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the support knowledge before using it">
    In the support mailbox's assistant, ask a factual question based on a policy
    you reviewed. For example:

    ```text
    Using this workspace's business information, summarize our published return
    policy. Include the source reference if available and identify any missing
    conditions. Do not infer whether a particular order qualifies, and do not
    send any email or change mailbox settings.
    ```

    Compare the answer with your current policy page. Check missing or outdated
    details before using the information in customer communication. Business
    information provides reference material, not access to an order's status or
    authority to approve a return.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review your first support reply

Keep **Draft for review** selected while you run this test:

1. From an external inbox you control, send a routine question to the support mailbox, such as a question about the published return window. Do not include private customer details.
2. Open the incoming message and review its prepared reply. If no draft appears, check the mailbox's reply settings; do not assume a reply was sent.
3. Check the recipient, policy facts, tone, signature, and any promises. Correct missing or unsupported details.
4. Send the reviewed reply yourself and confirm it reaches your test inbox.

Once you are satisfied with the reviewed replies, you can choose **Automatic
triage** for routine questions. Review topics and uncertain answers still need
your attention. A connected domain alone does not configure support replies.

## Check the outcome

- **Active business information** shows the intended store and is enabled in
  the correct workspace.
- Important business facts match the public source pages you reviewed.
- The support mailbox uses the applied signature in a new compose.
- The support mailbox shows the intended **Email response mode**, and topics
  you want to approve yourself are listed for review.
- A reviewed test reply reaches your external test inbox.
- Reloading the page retains the successfully applied signature.
- The unchecked partnerships mailbox keeps its own signature, while its agent
  still shares the workspace's business information.

Website setup and email delivery are separate. If customers cannot reach the
support mailbox, check its address and domain configuration rather than
re-importing the website.

## Keep the setup current

When your website policies change, use **Re-analyze**, review the new findings,
and apply the new result. Do not assume that editing a website page updates the
existing workspace profile automatically. Review signatures separately if
contact details have changed.

Use **Disable** when you want agents to stop using the retained context; it
does not turn off existing signatures. Use **Remove** when you want to remove
the profile and disable its associated website signatures, including edited
ones. Manually created signatures and sent emails are not
removed.

New accounts that sign up from the public
[Site Analytics page](https://mermail.app/site-analytics) also get a guided
**Set up customer support** window that covers business information, reply
mode (starting on **Automatic triage**), and an optional custom domain in one
place.

For detailed editor behavior, guided setup, and troubleshooting,
see the [Site Analytics guide](/guides/site-analytics). To choose a
different workflow, return to [Usecases overview](/usecases/overview).
