Find businesses that match your target audience, review their public contact
details, and introduce your product from a Mermail mailbox.

Start with contacts you already have permission to approach, or research a small
number of relevant businesses. Keep research, draft preparation, and send
approval as separate steps. A paid Mermail plan does not purchase prospect data
or authorize outreach automatically.

## Before you begin

- [Create a Mermail mailbox](/guides/create-mailbox) and confirm the workspace
  and sender address you want to use.
- If you use a branded address, complete [custom domain setup](/guides/custom-domains)
  and test both sending and receiving first.
- [Connect your assistant to Mermail MCP](/ai/mcp) if you want to use the prompts
  below. You can also review and send drafts in the Mermail web app.
- Prepare your product description, target audience, and a small contact list
  or clear research criteria. Exclude people who have opted out.

### Optional paid research

The [Apify outreach demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RsQPHgKApHw) shows a
paid research option. It is not required when you already have suitable contacts.
For wallet-enabled research, use the full OAuth MCP profile and
[connect Agent Wallet](/agent-wallet/get-started). The workspace owner completes
the initial PayBox connection; an API-key connection or agent-inbox profile does
not unlock Agent Wallet.

Check available research tools, current prices, and your spending limit before
approving a purchase. A Mermail subscription does not include third-party
research charges.

## Run the workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your sender mailbox">
    Ask your connected assistant to check Mermail and list your mailboxes:

    ```text
    Check my Mermail MCP connection and list the mailboxes I can use.
    Do not send any email or purchase anything yet.
    ```

    Select the exact mailbox and workspace from the result. Use that sender
    consistently for research context, saved emails, and any later send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define your prospect search">
    If you already have contacts, give the assistant your approved list and its
    source, then skip paid service access. Ask it to review the list without
    purchasing research or sending emails.

    For optional Apify research, make the selection criteria explicit instead
    of relying on an undefined ranking such as “top”:

    ```text
    Use the mailbox I selected for this outreach workflow.
    Find up to 3 Shopify businesses in Australia using Apify.
    Prioritize businesses with a public business contact address and a clear
    reason they could benefit from a customer support inbox.

    Show each business's name, website, public contact email, source URL,
    and the evidence for its relevance. Do not guess owner names or email
    addresses. Exclude duplicates and contacts who previously opted out.

    Check whether usable Apify access already exists. If a purchase is needed,
    show me the current cost and terms and wait for my approval. Do not send
    outreach yet.
    ```

    Public research may return fewer than three suitable contacts. Ask for an
    explanation rather than filling missing results with invented details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete service access if required">
    Skip this step if you are using your own contacts. Otherwise, review the proposed Apify purchase and set an explicit spending limit
    before authorizing it. If signing is required, follow the console signing
    link returned by the assistant and complete the approval in the app.

    The video shows the user asking for the console signing URL, then returning
    to the conversation after signing. You can use the same handoff:

    ```text
    Show me the console signing link for the pending purchase.
    Do not create another purchase.
    ```

    After signing, ask the assistant to check the existing payment and service
    access before continuing. Payment approval alone does not confirm that
    research has completed. See [x402 and paid services](/agent-wallet/swaps-and-x402)
    for the payment and recovery concepts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review leads and prepare emails">
    Check the returned businesses, source links, and recipient addresses.
    A public contact address is not proof that it belongs to the business owner.
    Approve the contacts you want to use, then ask for personalized emails:

    ```text
    Prepare one short outreach email for each approved contact from my selected
    Mermail mailbox. Introduce Mermail as a customer support inbox.
    Use only the product details I provided and the sourced business facts.
    Include a clear subject and a low-pressure invitation to learn more.
    Do not claim we have used their products or spoken before.
    Save the emails as drafts in Mermail and report which ones were saved.
    Do not send them yet.
    ```

    Open the saved emails in Mermail. Check the sender, recipient, subject,
    personalization, links, and signature. Include a clear way to decline
    further contact, and honor those requests before preparing follow-ups.
    Public contact details alone do not establish permission to send.
    Email preparation and sending stay in Mermail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve sending and verify the result">
    Once the content is ready, explicitly identify the reviewed drafts you want
    sent. For example:

    ```text
    Send only the two drafts I just reviewed and identified above, unchanged,
    from the selected mailbox. Leave all other drafts untouched.
    Report the result for each email.
    ```

    Check **Sent** in that mailbox and the returned send results. A saved draft
    is not a sent email, and a successful send does not guarantee delivery or
    a reply. If a send result is uncertain, inspect the mailbox before retrying
    to avoid contacting the same person twice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check the outcome

- The sender belongs to the intended workspace and mailbox.
- Every used contact has a source; missing contact details were not guessed.
- Any paid research stayed within your approved limit; existing contacts required no research purchase.
- Only the emails you approved were sent; unapproved drafts remain untouched.
- You can distinguish failed, saved, and sent results for each recipient.

## If the workflow stops

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Wallet or research tools are unavailable | Ask the assistant to inspect its available tools. Check the full OAuth MCP connection and service availability before proceeding. |
| PayBox needs connection or reauthorization | Complete the handoff in Mermail; ask the workspace owner if your role cannot authorize it. |
| Payment is pending | Check the existing payment and finish its signing step. Do not submit a duplicate purchase. |
| Apify returns no usable contact | Refine the criteria or skip that business. Do not infer an email address from a name or domain. |
| Emails exist only as drafts | Review them and explicitly approve the intended send. Research or draft creation does not authorize sending. |
